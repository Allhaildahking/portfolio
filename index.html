Trade-Oracle is one of my experiments in making market analysis more structured. The idea is to turn price action, multi-timeframe context, and risk-to-reward rules into a tool that helps evaluate a setup before a trade is taken. It doesn't execute trades. The goal is better decisions, not blind automation.
